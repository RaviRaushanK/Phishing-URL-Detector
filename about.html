Why phishing awareness matters

Phishing websites often copy trusted brands and pressure users into entering passwords, card details, or private data. A quick URL check can stop a risky click before it becomes a bigger problem.

Phishing sites can look almost identical to real websites.
Attackers often hide fake domains inside long URLs.
Urgent messages are used to make users click quickly.
Verifying URLs protects accounts and personal information.

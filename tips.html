Stay Safe Online

Use these quick checks before opening unknown links, entering passwords, or sharing personal information online.

Scan a URL

Quick rule

If a link asks for urgent action, money, login details, or private information, pause and verify the URL first.

Phishing Warning Signs

Strange URL

Look for misspelled brands, extra words, too many dots, or domains that do not match the real company.

Urgent Message

Be careful with messages that threaten account closure, payment failure, prize loss, or immediate action.

No HTTPS

Avoid entering passwords or payment details on pages that do not use a secure HTTPS connection.

Unexpected Sender

Verify emails, SMS messages, and social links that arrive unexpectedly, even when they look official.

Before you click

Hover and inspect

Check where the link really goes before opening it.

Use official websites

Type the website address yourself instead of trusting login links.

Protect passwords

Never enter credentials on a page you reached from a suspicious message.

Good habits

Enable two-factor login

Add another security layer to important accounts.

Report suspicious links

Reporting bad links helps protect other users too.

Scan unknown URLs

Use PhishShield when a link feels unusual or risky.
